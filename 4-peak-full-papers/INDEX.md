## Notes

- **2019, 2021-S1, 2021-S2 are image-only scans** — no selectable text, no search. Fine to
  print and solve; you cannot copy questions out of them.
- **Separate answer keys exist only for 2022, 2024, 2025, 2026.** For the earlier years the
  key pages are bundled at the end of the paper itself, and in any case every answer is in
  the GATE Overflow splits (`../2-build-core-W01-W16/`) and the uncut volumes
  (`../5-reference-full-volumes/`) — answers only, no worked explanations.
- **2027**: `gate.iitm.ac.in` currently redirects to `gate2027.iitm.ac.in`, which is not yet
  live. Check it for the official notification and exam dates.

## Suggested use

Save these for Phase 3 (Jan 2027). Solving them early wastes their only real value — a
clean, unseen, full-length paper under exam conditions. Two exceptions: sit 2024, 2025 and
2026 at the end of Phase 2 (week of 28 Dec) as a baseline.
