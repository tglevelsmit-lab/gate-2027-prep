## Question banks

The topic-wise question banks are cut from the **GATE Overflow** PDF compilation
(<https://gateoverflow.in>, released at <https://github.com/GATEOverflow/GO-PDFs>),
compiled by the GATE Overflow team led by Arjun Suresh. They are freely distributed by
their authors. Please support the original project rather than relying on this mirror.

## Official question papers

`4-peak-full-papers/` contains GATE Computer Science question papers and answer keys
released by the organising institutes (IISc and the IITs). **These carry an explicit
"Copyright © GATE" notice.** They are reproduced here for personal study only. See
`4-peak-full-papers/INDEX.md` for the source of each file.
